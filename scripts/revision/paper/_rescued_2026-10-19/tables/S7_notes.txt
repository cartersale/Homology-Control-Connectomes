S7_setlevel.tex: notes per table
=================================
Repo root: /Users/cartersale/Research/projects/higher_order_connectomes (read-only; nothing in it was modified)
Generator: scratchpad/scripts/make_s7.py (read-only pandas). All numbers are copied from saved CSVs unless a note says "computed".
Common settings: A = A0 - (lambda_max + c)I, infinite-horizon Gramian, eps = 1e-5 where the eps floor applies.
q1 = lambda1(W)/tr W. In the modal and q1 files, q1 is computed on the RAW W (no eps floor; q1/validation.json).
"ER" (effective_rank) = exp-entropy of the eps-floored spectrum. "er_raw" = the same without the floor.
None of the modal/* analyses include k = 1 (pairwise quantities are undefined). Exception: the L_S table, which uses set_q1.csv.gz.
